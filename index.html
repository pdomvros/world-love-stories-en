Love Stories of World History

Click the "Select Story" button to be given a random love story from World History along with the characteristics of its protagonists. Click the "Print" button to create a PDF file with the necessary information. Finally, click the "My View" button and write your opinion about the love story you were given.

Phrases for your opinion:

  • In my opinion, the story of… is a timeless example of true love.
  • I believe that the relationship between … and … shows how love can change history.
  • Their love was so strong that it overcame the social barriers of their time.
  • Although their story is romantic, I think their choices were reckless.
  • The decision of … to abandon everything for … is impressive but also controversial.
  • I am not sure if their love was genuine or if it was influenced by their political ambitions.
  • The tragic ending of their story proves that love is not always happy.
  • I believe that … was more devoted to the relationship, while … was influenced by other factors.
  • Their story shows how difficult it was to be in love in that era.
  • If they lived today, I think … and … would not face so many difficulties.
  • The way … sacrificed … for their love deeply moves me.
  • Although they are considered romantic heroes, I believe their actions had negative consequences.
  • Their relationship seems more like a story of passion than a stable love.
  • … should have chosen personal happiness over …
  • Their love was doomed from the start due to social conditions.
  • The attitude of … towards … shows how love can be a source of strength but also weakness.
  • Can we compare this story to modern romantic relationships?
  • Personally, I think this story is more of a myth than a real love.
  • The way their love is described reflects the culture and values of their time.
